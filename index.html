01 / Concept

Real production.
Useful evidence.

Start with the farm.

Before a digital asset can represent anything meaningful, the underlying production needs to be observable. Leafcoin starts with growing conditions, crop records and the quality of the data.

Grow with better visibility

Bring temperature, humidity, light, pH and nutrient readings together. Help operators spot changing conditions and make informed adjustments.

Trace every batch

Connect seed origin, growing conditions, crop cycles and harvest records in a product passport that can be accessed through a QR code.

Verify the record

Keep the detailed data in controlled storage. Anchor report fingerprints on a ledger so readers can check whether a record has changed.

Leafcoin is a proposed ecosystem. The public lab materials document personal prototypes; they do not establish a deployed farm network, issued token or licensed financial service.

02 / Architecture

From a reading
to a verifiable record.

A data layer before a token layer.

The proposed flow separates collection, storage and verification. Sensitive telemetry stays off-chain; the ledger holds evidence of the record’s integrity.

  1. 01MeasureFarm sensors
  2. 02ValidateQuality checks
  3. 03StoreOff-chain data
  4. 04VerifyReport hashes
  5. 05UseAI & passports
Collection & data qualityIoT

Timestamp readings and transport them through MQTT. Flag missing samples, outliers and calibration issues before they enter reports or training data. Keep crop-cycle and device context alongside each reading.

Storage & integrityLedger

Store raw telemetry in an access-controlled repository. Periodically calculate a report’s cryptographic hash and record it on a ledger. A matching hash shows that the report has not changed since it was recorded; it does not prove the sensor reading was correct.

The original concept proposes GoQuorum. The ledger choice, operating model and verification design would need evaluation during the MVP.

Recommendations & traceabilityAI / QR

Use validated data to explore forecasts and cultivation recommendations, with an operator reviewing actions before they affect the farm. Version datasets and models so recommendations can be investigated.

A batch passport would bring together seed origin, growing conditions, nutrient records, crop-cycle dates and harvest history. Public summaries and private operational data would have separate access rules.

Future digital-asset integrationsResearch

Explore whether verified production records could support asset-linked services. Token design, asset rights, custody, valuation and redemption remain research questions.

Any financial integration would depend on legal review and suitable licensed partners. Thailand is the target context of the original concept; no regulatory approval or partner participation is claimed.

03 / Roadmap

Build in stages.
Validate each step.

A staged path from lab to ecosystem.

A proposed sequence, with progress tied to evidence rather than fixed launch dates.

  1. 01

    Telemetry MVP

    Connect a pilot farm, validate readings and build monitoring views. Test report fingerprints and a basic batch passport.

    Validate: sensor coverage, data quality and record verification.

  2. 02

    Supervised AI

    Evaluate forecasts and recommendations against recorded crop outcomes. Keep operators in control of cultivation changes.

    Validate: model quality, reproducibility and operator review.

  3. 03

    Regulated integrations

    Research asset-linked services and partner requirements. Define legal responsibilities and user rights before building financial flows.

    Validate: feasibility, partner scope and the regulatory path.

  4. 04

    Additional farms & crops

    Extend the system only after the pilot is useful and repeatable. Adapt monitoring and models to new sites and crops.

    Validate: repeatable operations and sustainable unit economics.
